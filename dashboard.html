      Frequently asked questions

      How do I start an instant meeting?

      Click the New meeting button on the Overview page. Choose "Start instant" to begin immediately, or "Schedule for later" to plan ahead.

      How do I invite someone to a meeting?

      After creating a meeting, click Copy invite link and share it. Anyone with the link can join directly — no account needed.

      Where can I see past meetings?

      Go to Meeting History in the sidebar. You'll find all your recent and missed meetings listed there.

      How do I change my display name?

      Head to Settings in the sidebar, update your Display name, and click Save changes.
